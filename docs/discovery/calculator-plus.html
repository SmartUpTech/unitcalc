Calculations and Graphy

Work with arithmetic, parentheses, percentages, powers and square roots. Graphy explains supported calculations visually and exports diagrams as PDF or PNG.

Units and currency

Convert length, area, mass, temperature, volume, speed, time and other supported units. Search unit names and symbols. Currency rates can be refreshed with internet access; the app shows their update status. Rates are for reference, and transaction rates and fees may differ.

History, themes and tools

Inspect and restore saved calculations, choose themes, adjust decimal and grouping display and play hosted daily games. Some themes and extra game sets use optional rewarded ads.

Common questions

Does currency refresh need internet access?

Yes. Cached rates may remain available, but their timestamp should be checked before use.

What does optional usage analytics include?

Feature, theme and game usage through Google Analytics for Firebase when enabled in Settings. Expressions, amounts, scores and saved history are excluded from these usage events.
